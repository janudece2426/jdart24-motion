# Home Surface Brief

## Direction contract

**THESIS**  
The work demonstrates the service immediately. The page refuses a generic marketing hero that asks visitors to trust claims before seeing motion.

**OWN-WORLD**  
A precise gallery system: true white and near-black surfaces, strong Korean sans typography, fine neutral hairlines, restrained rounded media, original portfolio color, and one controlled studio-light motion.

**STORY**  
Visitors first understand that JDART24 adds motion to ecommerce detail pages, then learn the two production methods, browse every category, open GIF or WebP results, and retain a unique design identifier for inquiry.

**FIRST VIEWPORT**  
Stable copy occupies a protected left zone. On desktop, three distinct portfolio works form a depth composition on the right; the center work is animated and dominant, while the supporting works are quieter. A slow light field supports the media without entering the text zone. Mobile keeps the copy first and shows one dominant work beneath it.

**FORM**  
Experience-led portfolio with a comp-first hero comparison. seed: none (unavailable).

**FINISH**  
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
